Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026. Applies to the Inkout extension for Chrome, version 1.0 and later.

In short

Inkout does not collect, store remotely or send any personal data. Everything it does happens inside your browser, on your device. It makes no network requests, has no account, and contains no analytics, tracking or advertising.

What Inkout reads, and why

To hide sensitive text, Inkout reads the text of the web pages you open, including what you type into text fields and the page's title. It looks for email addresses, phone numbers, card numbers, API keys, bank account numbers, IP addresses (if you turn that on) and any words you added yourself, and paints over them on screen. This reading happens on your device, as the page is shown. Page content is never saved and never leaves your browser.

To notice when you start sharing your screen, Inkout watches for a page starting a screen share through the browser. It only learns that a share started or stopped, and on which site; it never sees or records the shared video.

What Inkout stores

  • Your settings (what to hide, your custom words, the hide style, site rules, presenter-mode switches) are saved with Chrome's own sync storage. If you're signed in to Chrome with sync on, Chrome copies them to your other browsers through your Google account, as it does for other extension settings. Inkout has no server and never receives them.
  • Per-tab state (how many values are hidden on each tab, which tabs are paused, and which tab is sharing) is kept in Chrome's session storage. It stays on your device and is cleared when you close Chrome.

You can remove everything Inkout stores by removing the extension.

Permissions

  • Storage: to save your settings and the per-tab state described above.
  • Access to the sites you visit: sensitive text can appear on any website, so Inkout's hiding script runs on every page you open. It uses this access only to find and hide text on screen.

Sharing with others

Inkout does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone, because it has none to share. It uses no third-party services. The project website you're reading also loads nothing from other sites.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. The full history is public in the project's source code repository.
