Three things to set up, once

  1. 1 Pin it to the toolbar Click the puzzle-piece icon next to the address bar, then the pin next to Inkout. You will always see whether it is on.
  2. 2 Learn the shortcuts Alt + Shift + H turns protection on or off from any tab; a red OFF badge shows when it is off. Alt + Shift + K blurs the whole page.
  3. 3 Pick what to hide Emails, phones, cards, API keys and bank account numbers are on. Add your own words in settings.
